# Editorial Research

## Job to Be Done

Research and challenge a `Candidate` before drafting.

Use this skill in Editorial Studio.

The goal is not to collect links. The goal is to determine whether the thesis survives evidence.

## Inputs

Required:
- Content Item ID
- candidate title
- core idea / proposed thesis
- BA implication

Helpful:
- known sources
- series
- saturation assessment
- prior related posts

## Source Priority

Prefer:
1. first-party product/company documentation or research
2. analyst firms and standards bodies
3. academic research
4. established consulting/research organizations
5. strong industry publications
6. high-quality practitioner evidence where appropriate

Prefer recent sources when the claim is time-sensitive.

## Workflow

1. Restate the thesis as a falsifiable or challengeable claim.
2. Identify the claims that require evidence.
3. Gather credible supporting evidence.
4. Search for contradictory or limiting evidence.
5. Separate:
   - verified fact
   - reasonable inference
   - editorial interpretation
6. Test whether the proposed BA implication actually follows.
7. Check saturation and competing narratives.
8. Refine, narrow, or reject the thesis.
9. Identify the strongest practical implication for a senior BA.
10. Record unresolved evidence gaps.

## Output Contract

Return:
- Content Item ID
- Research question
- Thesis before research
- Key evidence
- Counter-evidence / limitations
- What is verified
- What is interpretation
- Revised thesis
- Senior BA implication
- Practical insight
- Evidence gaps
- Source list
- Research readiness:
  - Ready to Draft
  - Needs More Evidence
  - Rethink Thesis

Do not mark the item `Approved`.

## Quality Checks

- Are the strongest claims source-backed?
- Were counterpoints considered?
- Are facts distinguished from interpretation?
- Did the research materially sharpen the thesis?
- Is the BA implication more than a generic productivity statement?
