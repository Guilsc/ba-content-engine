# Post Learning

## Job to Be Done

Convert real post-performance data into evidence-based editorial learning.

Use this skill after publication.

Do not overfit small samples.

## Required Resources

Use:
- `post-performance.md`
- `content-strategy.md`
- relevant published post text
- prior performance records when available

## Inputs

Required:
- Content Item ID
- post text
- publication date/time
- available performance metrics

Helpful:
- qualitative comments
- profile visits
- relevant DMs
- recruiter/peer interest
- comparison posts

## Workflow

1. Record the available metrics without inventing missing values.
2. Evaluate the quality of response, not only reach.
3. Compare against relevant prior posts when the sample allows.
4. Look for patterns in:
   - hook
   - topic
   - content type
   - length
   - timing
   - saves/sends
   - comments
   - profile actions
   - quality of discussion
5. Label confidence:
   - Low
   - Moderate
   - High
6. Separate observation from causal claim.
7. Identify what should be retained, tested, stopped, or changed next.
8. Feed only credible learnings back into future strategy.

## Output Contract

Return:

**KEEP**
What appears to work.

**TEST**
What deserves another controlled experiment.

**STOP**
What repeatedly underperforms or weakens positioning.

**NEXT**
What should change in upcoming items.

Also include:
- Confidence
- Evidence limitations
- Strategy update recommended: Yes / No

## Quality Checks

- Is the sample too small for a strong claim?
- Are you confusing correlation with causation?
- Did you prioritize relevant professional engagement over vanity metrics?
- Is every recommendation tied to observed evidence?
