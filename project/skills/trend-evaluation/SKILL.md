# Trend Evaluation

## Job to Be Done

Evaluate an external signal or Trend Scout finding for editorial relevance to senior Business Analysts.

Use this skill in the Trend Radar stage.

Do not draft a post and do not automatically promote an item to Candidate.

## Inputs

Required:
- trend, announcement, paper, product change, or signal
- available sources/evidence

Helpful:
- publication date
- source authority
- existing BA Content Engine backlog
- current watchlist

## Workflow

1. Identify the actual change, not the marketing headline.
2. Explain **Why Now**.
3. Identify the direct BA impact.
4. Identify the **second-order implication** for senior BA work.
5. Assess evidence strength:
   - Strong
   - Moderate
   - Weak
6. Assess saturation:
   - Low
   - Medium
   - High
7. Check overlap with existing backlog themes.
8. Identify the strongest possible thought-leadership angle.
9. Assign editorial potential:
   - Watch
   - Explore
   - Candidate
10. Do not promote to Candidate without explicit user approval.

## Output Contract

Return:

- Signal
- Why Now
- BA Impact
- Second-order Implication
- Evidence Strength
- Saturation
- Existing Backlog Overlap
- Strongest Editorial Angle
- Editorial Potential
- Recommendation state:
  - PROMOTE?
  - WATCH
  - IGNORE

`PROMOTE?` is a question for the user, not an automatic transition.

## Quality Checks

Before completing:
- Did the analysis go beyond “AI is changing work”?
- Is the BA implication specific?
- Is evidence quality explicit?
- Did saturation affect the conclusion?
- Did you avoid drafting the post?
- Did you avoid silently changing lifecycle status?
