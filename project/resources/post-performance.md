# LinkedIn Post Performance

## Purpose

Track real post performance so future editorial decisions are based on Guilherme's own evidence rather than generic LinkedIn advice.

## Per-Post Record

For each published post capture:

- Content Item ID
- Date
- Time
- Series
- Topic
- Content type
- Format
- Hook
- Approximate length
- Impressions
- Reactions
- Comments
- Reposts
- Saves
- Sends
- Profile visits
- New followers
- Relevant DMs or recruiter interest
- Qualitative discussion quality
- Notes
- Full post text

## Learning Principles

Compare:
- stronger vs weaker hooks
- topic performance
- content types
- conceptual vs practical posts
- personal experience vs abstract analysis
- short vs long posts
- publication timing
- comments relative to reactions
- saves and sends
- profile visits
- relevant new followers
- quality of discussion
- whether the intended thesis was understood

Do not claim strong patterns from a tiny sample.

Use confidence labels:
- Low
- Moderate
- High

## Learning Output

End each review with:

**KEEP**
What appears to work.

**TEST**
What deserves another experiment.

**STOP**
What repeatedly underperforms or dilutes positioning.

**NEXT**
What should change in upcoming content.
