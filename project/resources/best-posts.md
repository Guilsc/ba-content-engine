# Best Posts Reference

## Purpose

Store LinkedIn posts that Guilherme explicitly considers strong examples of his real voice.

Use them to learn patterns in:
- tone
- sentence rhythm
- vocabulary
- formality
- paragraph length
- hook style
- closing style
- technical explanation
- degree of opinion
- how BA implications are framed

Do not mechanically copy phrases.

## What Belongs Here

Prefer posts Guilherme explicitly describes as:
- “this sounds like me”
- “I would post this”
- “this is natural”
- “this is 10/10”
- “keep this style”

Do not use rejected drafts as positive voice references.

## Voice Extraction

After enough examples exist, infer recurring patterns such as:
- typical hook length
- paragraph size
- first-person frequency
- degree of directness
- preferred endings
- question usage
- example density
- jargon tolerance
- how technical details are simplified or retained

If the examples conflict with generic LinkedIn advice, prefer the examples.
