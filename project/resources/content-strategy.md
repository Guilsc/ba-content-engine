# BA Content Engine — Content Strategy

## Objective

Build credible thought leadership around Business Analysis at the intersection of enterprise technology, AI, data, decision-making, automation, and the future of work.

The goal is professional positioning through useful analysis, not generic influencer growth.

## Editorial Differentiator

Do not merely explain technology trends.

Translate them into:
- changing decision rights
- new requirements responsibilities
- governance needs
- semantic risks
- process redesign
- human-agent operating models
- evidence and measurement needs
- implications for senior BA practice

The recurring question is:

**What does this actually change for a senior Business Analyst?**

## Priority Themes

High-priority themes include:
- Business Analysis in the Agentic Era
- decision quality and decision intelligence
- business semantics and semantic layers
- agent governance and decision rights
- agent-native workflow design
- AI-enabled BI
- context engineering
- human-agent collaboration
- process redesign
- requirements for autonomous or semi-autonomous systems
- measurable business value
- enterprise integration viewed through BA decisions

## Content Types

Strong formats:
- OPINION — a defensible professional position
- PROOF — a real example, result, pattern, or observation
- TEACH — something a senior BA can apply
- FRAMEWORK — a structured way to think or work
- EXPERIMENT — a tested method, especially involving AI
- STORY — a real professional situation with a useful takeaway
- TREND — a current signal interpreted through a BA lens
- CAREER — a lesson shaped by cross-functional technology experience

## Topic Quality Test

Before promoting an idea:
- Why now?
- Is there a real point of view?
- What is the non-obvious BA implication?
- Is there credible evidence?
- How saturated is the topic?
- Does it strengthen Guilherme's professional positioning?
- Could another generic AI/BA creator publish this unchanged?

If the last answer is yes, sharpen or discard the idea.

## Evidence

Prefer:
- first-party technology sources
- analyst firms
- academic research
- standards and specifications
- strong industry publications
- real professional observations that can be safely shared

A trend with weak evidence stays in Watch rather than being forced into content.

## Series

Series are useful when:
- there is a genuine conceptual sequence
- every post stands alone
- each post adds a distinct angle
- the series is not artificially stretched

Current flagship series:
**Business Analysis in the Agentic Era**

## Performance

Do not optimize only for impressions.

Better signals include:
- thoughtful comments
- saves
- sends/shares
- profile visits
- relevant new followers
- recruiter or peer interest
- quality of discussion
- evidence that the intended professional audience understood the thesis

Use the post-learning workflow to update future editorial choices from real performance.
