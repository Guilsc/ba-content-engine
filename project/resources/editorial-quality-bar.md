# Editorial Quality Bar

A BA Content Engine item is strong when it has:

1. A current or durable trigger worth discussing.
2. A clear thesis, not merely a topic.
3. A second-order implication for senior Business Analysts.
4. Credible evidence proportionate to the claim.
5. A distinct angle relative to saturated LinkedIn/AI commentary.
6. Practical relevance to decisions, governance, process, semantics, requirements, or measurable value.
7. Language that sounds natural and senior.
8. No invented facts or confidential client detail.
9. A visual concept that reinforces the thesis when a visual is used.
10. A closing thought that advances the argument rather than asking for generic engagement.

A weak item should be sharpened, held, or discarded rather than pushed through the pipeline.
